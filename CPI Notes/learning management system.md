### 1. **Moodle**
- **Certificate Generation**: Yes, Moodle allows certificate creation through plugins such as the **Custom Certificate** or **Simple Certificate**. These plugins let you customize and issue certificates automatically upon course completion.
- **Link**: Moodle Plugins
### 2. **Chamilo**
- **Certificate Generation**: Yes, Chamilo has built-in support for **certificate generation**. Administrators can issue certificates to learners after course completion, and these can be customized to suit your organization’s needs.
- **Link**: [Chamilo](https://chamilo.org/)
### 3. **Open edX**
- **Certificate Generation**: Yes, Open edX supports **certificate generation** for course completion. Certificates can be customized and issued automatically. It's often used by universities for verified certification programs.
- **Link**: [Open edX](https://openedx.org)
### 4. **Forma LMS**
- **Certificate Generation**: Yes, Forma LMS supports **automatic certificate issuance** upon completion of a course. You can design your certificates and customize them for your learners.
- **Link**: [Forma LMS](https://www.formalms.org)
### 5. **ILIAS**
- **Certificate Generation**: Yes, ILIAS supports **certificate generation** for learners who complete courses. It also allows administrators to customize the content and layout of certificates.
- **Link**: [ILIAS](https://www.ilias.de)

All of these LMS platforms offer **certificate generation**, making them suitable for both corporate training and academic environments where certification is important.
